# Encore

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individuals managing international travel around concerts, primarily in Japan.

## Product Purpose

Keep concert applications, deadlines, results, payments, trips, and hotel stays together.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Frontend prototype with fictional browser-local data. JPY primary, USD/AUD/SGD kept separate. Discord authentication, actual reminder delivery, scheduling, and provider parsing are future integrations. Preserve working document navigation and persist changes before redirects.

## Brand Commitments

The user authorized a complete visual remake, specified modern iOS-inspired glass, rejected generic dashboard styling, and requested Impeccable. Existing conversation establishes the audience, workflows and permission to implement; do not repeat the approval interview.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing domain functions, fixtures, forms, tests, and public/tokyo.jpg (source credit in README). The generated conversation concept establishes a cool native-app direction, not factual event information.

## Product Principles

Deadlines before decoration. Clear time zones and original currencies. Maintainable modules. Honest demo boundaries.
